Building a Corporate Stationery System That Lasts

A cohesive stationery system — business cards, letterheads, envelopes, invoice books and diaries that all feel like they belong to the same brand — does more for a company's credibility than most business owners realise. It's often the first tangible touchpoint a client has with your business, before a single product or service is delivered.

The starting point is always the business card, because its size constraints force clarity: your logo, name, designation and contact details have to work in a 3.5 x 2 inch space before anything else is designed. Once that's settled, the same colour palette, paper stock and finishing technique should carry through to every other piece.

Letterheads deserve more thought than they usually get. We recommend keeping the header simple — logo and one line of contact information — and leaving generous white space for the actual letter content. A letterhead that's too busy competes with whatever is typed onto it later.

For invoice books, bill books and receipt books, durability and carbon-copy quality matter more than decoration. We use carbonless paper that copies cleanly through three layers, with your business details pre-printed and sequential numbering built in, so nothing needs to be handwritten beyond the transaction itself.

The biggest mistake we see growing businesses make is treating stationery as a one-time purchase rather than a system. Order your business cards, letterheads and diaries together, from the same press, using the same specifications file — that way, when you need a reprint eight months from now, everything still matches exactly.
