Inside Our Ahmedabad Press

Bhagwati Card Agency has operated from the same Chandni Chowk-inspired press setup for close to four decades, though our current Ahmedabad studio has grown considerably since those early years. Walking through the floor today, you'll find foil stamping machines running alongside laser cutters, offset presses next to hand-finishing tables where artisans thread ribbon closures by hand.

Every order that comes through our doors — whether it's fifty wedding cards or fifty thousand corporate letterheads — passes through the same core stages: design proofing, paper selection, plate or die preparation, printing, and finishing. What changes with scale is the equipment used, not the care taken at each step.

Our finishing team is where a lot of the craftsmanship customers notice actually happens. Foil stamping and offset printing get a card 80% of the way there, but the final assembly — scoring and folding by hand, attaching inserts, tying ribbons, packing each piece individually — is done by artisans who've often been with us for over a decade.

We've also invested steadily in newer capabilities over the years: laser cutting for intricate jaali and mandala patterns, UV printing for raised, glossy detail on dark card stock, and digital printing for shorter runs and rapid sampling before a full production order is confirmed.

What hasn't changed is our approach to quality checks. Every batch is checked by hand before packing — colour consistency, foil adhesion, cut precision — because a printing defect that's invisible on a screen becomes very visible in a guest's hand at a wedding reception. That's a standard we've held since 1987, and it's the one thing that scale will never be allowed to compromise.
